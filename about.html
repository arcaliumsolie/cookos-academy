About Cooko

Our students come to Cooko Academy not only to pursue their barista dreams, but to experience fun, hands-on learning, expert mentorship, and a supportive community that helps them become excellent and successful in their craft or future business.
From steaming milk to latte art, from theory to actual café setup, we make sure every class is a step closer to your goal.

We uphold fundamental, focused and fun learning experience for the students to become excellent and successful in their profession or business by encouraging and giving them support to dream for their future and the industry’s.

Our Mission

To provide focused, practical, and enjoyable learning that supports students in developing their skills and pursuing their goals in the coffee industry.

Our Vision

To help students become confident and successful professionals and entrepreneurs through quality training, mentorship, and hands-on experience.
