FUNDAMENTALS

Barista 101

Learn the essentials of coffee from bean to cup: green bean history, hand roasting, brewing methods, espresso machine operation, and menu making, capped with TESDA Barista NC II Assessment practice.

WHAT’S COVERED?

  • Coffee Fundamentals and Espresso Basics
  • Milk texturing and basic latte art
  • Manual and machine brewing methods
  • Cup tasting and flavor evaluation
  • Barista workstation and hygiene standards

Barista 102

Build on your foundation with intermediate bean knowledge, roasting machine use, advanced brewing techniques, and latte art, plus food safety, barista professionalism, and International Barista Assessment (KCF) preparation.

WHAT’S COVERED?

  • Fundamental attitude as a barista
  • Intermediate knowledge about coffee beans
  • Use of roasting machine (different stages)
  • Advanced brewing techniques
  • Intermediate latte art skills
